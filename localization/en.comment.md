# en.json

The English interface catalog, and the fallback for every missing key.
It is embedded into the veneer and read by `LLocalization` under the `localization.` prefix.

## `Terms.*`

A term is one reusable word, such as the product name, cited by many texts.

## Other keys

Keys are the resource names markup binds to, grouped by a dotted prefix per area.
Every other catalog carries the same keys.
